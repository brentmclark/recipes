# Quick Refrigerator Pickles

**Author:** Dillon Mulroy ([@dillon_mulroy](https://x.com/dillon_mulroy))  
**Source:** https://x.com/dillon_mulroy/status/2036241031071739985

### Ingredients (makes 2 × 32oz jars)
- 4–5 medium cucumbers (or enough to fill two 32oz jars)
- 2 cups white vinegar
- 2 cups water
- 2 Tbsp kosher salt
- 2 Tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp MSG (optional but recommended for extra umami)
- Chili flakes (optional, to taste)

### Method
1. Slice the cucumbers into spears, coins, or your preferred shape.
2. Pack the cucumber slices tightly into two clean 32oz jars.
3. In a saucepan or microwave-safe bowl, combine the vinegar, water, salt, sugar, and MSG.
4. Heat the brine until the salt and sugar are fully dissolved (no need to boil).
5. Pour the hot brine over the cucumbers, making sure they are completely submerged. Add chili flakes if desired.
6. Let the jars cool to room temperature, then seal and refrigerate.
7. The pickles are ready to eat after **1 hour** in the fridge. They keep for several weeks.

Enjoy your crunchy, flavorful quick pickles!
